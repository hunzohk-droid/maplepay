Frequently asked questions

How much tax is deducted from my paycheque in BC?

It depends on your income. In 2026, BC tax starts at 5.6% up to $50,363, plus federal tax from 14%, plus CPP (5.95%) and EI (1.63%). Enter your pay above for an estimated breakdown.

What is the BC basic personal amount for 2026?

$13,216 — the first $13,216 you earn is free from BC tax, worth a $740.10 credit. Federally it's $16,452.

What is CPP2?

An extra 4% CPP on earnings between $74,600 and $85,000. It applies when your pensionable earnings exceed the first CPP earnings ceiling.

Why is my actual paycheque different from this estimate?

Your employer uses your TD1 claims (spouse, dependants, other credits), which no calculator can know. Actual deductions can also differ because of payroll rounding, year-to-date contributions, taxable benefits, pension contributions and other adjustments. This calculator is a simplified annual estimate, not an exact payroll calculation.

How to use this BC salary calculator

Enter your gross pay in Canadian dollars before deductions. Choose hourly, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly or annual pay, then select how often you receive a paycheque. For hourly pay, enter your usual hours per week.

Bi-weekly pay means 26 payments per year. Semi-monthly pay means 24 payments per year. The annual estimate is divided by your selected number of payments; actual payroll deductions may vary throughout the year.

About MaplePay and this estimate

MaplePay is a free calculator for estimating employment take-home pay in British Columbia. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with the Canada Revenue Agency or the Government of British Columbia.

The simplified calculation applies the displayed tax brackets and basic personal credits, then subtracts CPP and EI. It does not model every credit, deduction or payroll adjustment, including CPP/EI tax credits, enhanced CPP deductions, income-tested personal amounts and the BC tax reduction. Results may overstate tax and should be checked against official payroll tools.

For an official comparison, use the CRA Payroll Deductions Online Calculator.
